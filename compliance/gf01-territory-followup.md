## Why this is needed

The enquiry as sent asks about **classification** — whether the stack meets
the mass-market criteria, and how to answer Apple's export-compliance
declaration. It does not ask **where the app may lawfully be distributed**.

Benji's stated intent (27 Aug) is worldwide App Store availability. That is a
distinct legal question and the opinion may not cover it unless asked:

- Apple's territory selector **defaults to all 175 territories**, so shipping
  without deselecting is an affirmative act, not an omission.
- Sanctioned destinations must be excluded as a matter of law, independent of
  whether the app is classified as mass-market.
- Some jurisdictions restrict or prohibit E2EE messaging or VoIP outright, or
  require local registration.

Cheap to add now while the opinion is being written; expensive to bolt on
afterwards.
